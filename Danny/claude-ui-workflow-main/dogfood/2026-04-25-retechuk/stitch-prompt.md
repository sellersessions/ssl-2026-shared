# Stitch prompt — Re Tech UK homepage redesign

> **Paste from this line down ↓**

Design a homepage for **Re Tech UK**, an everyday women's clothing brand selling knits, polo necks, cardigans and tees. The audience is UK women, broadly 25–45, shopping for considered everyday pieces that fit a multitasking life with kids and work.

**The feel:** feminine, editorial, warm, considered. Soft without being saccharine. Editorial polish over template energy. The kind of styling that says "someone who knows fashion put this together" — not "we picked a template." Designed for women buying for themselves.

**Visual signature:** light theme on a pale, paper-grain off-white background — keep that texture, do not flatten to solid. Scatter soft pastel petal blurs (warm purple, buttery yellow, peach, dusty pink) as decorative atmosphere across the page. Hand-drawn organic circle motifs as line-work accents. Photography is candid, outdoor, sunlit — women in soft pastel knits in natural settings. No packshots. No stock photography.

**Sections, in order:**

1. Hero — brand promise + a single styled photograph of a woman in a hero garment. Multi-CTA, not single conversion. Primary: "Shop new arrivals". Secondary: "Read our story".
2. Featured collections strip — Knits / Polos / Cardigans / Tees, four entry tiles.
3. Brand story strip — *"Everyday clothing for women who work with no boundaries."*
4. Trust strip — free UK delivery + free returns.
5. New arrivals grid (3 or 4 columns, generous spacing).
6. Editorial lookbook moment — full-bleed styled image, prose-led caption.
7. Footer with secondary CTA — *Read our story*.

**Avoid:** countdown timers, fake scarcity, urgency banners, single-CTA-above-fold, stock photography, kids clothing on the homepage, rewards / loyalty mentions, cool-grey corporate palette, sans-only type with no editorial voice.

**Type:** open to your suggestion. Current site uses Montserrat ExtraBold throughout — that reads industrial-tech, not editorial. Try a pairing: a feminine-leaning serif or display-serif for hero/headlines, a clean modern sans for body. Surprise me.

**Primary CTA colour:** open to your suggestion. Current site uses pure black for primary CTAs which feels harsh against the pastel decoration. Try something warmer — a deep aubergine, a warm brown, or sample from the petal palette.
