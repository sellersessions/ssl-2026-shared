step 1 · the URL

What's the URL?

Welcome to Claude UI Workflow. A local pipeline that turns a URL and a short brief into a polished landing page in 10 stages.

Claude handles stages 1 to 7 on its own: reading your URL, extracting brand tokens, building the design brief, then handing off to Stitch.

You step in at stage 7 to drive Stitch, and again at stage 8 to review the output.

Drop the URL we should learn from. For a redesign, use the existing site. For a brand refresh, use the same. For a fresh build, you can skip.

step 2 · the intent

What are you building?

A sentence or two is enough. Claude uses this to shape the rest of the questions it asks.

Be honest about the goal, not the polish. "I want a hero that pops" is fine. "I want CSS keyframe animation on the hero" is not what this field is for.

Mention the audience if it's not obvious from the URL. Mention the vibe if you have one in mind. Skip the rest.
