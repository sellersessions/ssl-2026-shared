How she works
Florence is built on the shoulders of:
  • 100+ hours of CRO methodology — distilled from Keplo's client work + Matt Kostan's 2026 ProductPinion interview
  • 17 ProductPinion test templates — calibration data for what actually lifts CTR / CVR
  • Three creative-director references — main image (1:1), listing slots (1:1), A+ Content (16:9), each with a 13-step canonical flow
  • 52-tactic main-image library — every variant Florence renders cites a specific tactic by number
  • Claude Cowork as the runtime — plugs into Slack, Notion, Calendar, ProductPinion, Higgsfield, your own MCPs (custom or official)
How that translates: when you type optimize-listing B07X, Florence chains 5 SellerApp endpoints, triangulates objections from reviews + Rufus, scores the keyword pool against the metric-to-action quadrant matrix, cites the exact tactic from the library — and emits a portable research artifact you can hand to a designer. Your brain stays current; the next skill picks up where this one left off.
