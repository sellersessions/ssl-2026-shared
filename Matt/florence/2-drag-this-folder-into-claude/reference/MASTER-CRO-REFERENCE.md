## 1. CRO Philosophy

Amazon listing optimization is not design work — it's conversion engineering. Every visual, every word, every pixel must be justified by customer data and measured by its impact on conversion.

**The fundamental chain:** Research → Strategy → Execution → Testing → Iteration. No step can be skipped. Content created without research is guesswork. Content that isn't tested is assumption.

**Core belief:** The customer tells you what your listing should look like. Not a template, not a competitor, not a designer's instinct. Reviews, keywords, Rufus queries, and competitor gaps — these four data sources contain the blueprint for every image, every A+ segment, every copy decision. Your job is to extract and execute.

**The non-negotiable principles that govern all visual content:**

1. **Show Me, Don't Tell Me** — Lead with photography that visually demonstrates the benefit. If you have to explain with text, the image is failing. The photograph IS the proof.
2. **Photography First, Copy Second** — 70%+ photography, copy is 3-6 words per callout (labels, not explanations).
3. **Clean, Focused, Scannable** — One message per image. Communicate in under 2 seconds. Billboard test.
4. **Right Demographic** — Person in image must match target customer. Photo reviews are ground truth.
5. **Mobile-First** — 80%+ browse on mobile. All text legible at phone size (24pt+). Test at 400x400px.
6. **Visual Consistency** — Same fonts, colors, style across gallery + A+. Designed as a set.

**There is no template.** Every product gets a research-driven, custom content strategy. The research brief decides the image stack — not a formula.

## 2. Research Framework

### The 4 Research Sources

Every visual content decision must be backed by data from these four sources:

**1. Review Mining** — The primary signal. Extract purchase drivers (why they buy), positive surprises (untapped selling points), complaints (objections to preempt), repeated questions (content gaps), comparison mentions (competitive positioning), and photo reviews (demographic truth). Mine all star ratings — 1-3 stars for objections, 4 stars for nuanced feedback, 5 stars for purchase drivers.

**2. Keyword & Search Intent** — What shoppers type reveals what they care about. High-volume benefit keywords = mandatory visual content. Feature-specific searches = that feature matters to buyers. Problem-based searches = show the solution. Long-tail qualifiers = lifestyle photography direction ("for small apartments" = show it in a small space).

**3. Rufus AI Queries** — What customers ask Amazon's AI. Every Rufus question = a gap in your listing's information architecture. FAQ questions → A+ bottom half. Comparison questions → listing image comparisons. Use case questions → lifestyle imagery. Concern questions → objection-handling images.

**4. Competitor Audit** — Screenshot and analyze top 5-10 competitors. Identify table stakes (must match), quality benchmarks (must meet or exceed), and differentiation gaps (opportunity to own). Main image grid comparison reveals scroll-stop opportunity.

### The Research Brief

Synthesize all four sources into five answers:
1. Who is the customer? (Demographics, context, lifestyle)
2. Why are they buying? (Top 3-5 purchase drivers, ranked)
3. What are they worried about? (Top 3-5 objections/questions)
4. What's the competitive landscape? (Table stakes, gaps, differentiation)
5. What benefits must be shown visually? (Prioritized list driving slot assignments)

**Rule: No image should be created without a clear link back to the research brief.**

## 3. Visual Content System

### Main Image (Slot 1) — Stop the Scroll

**Job:** Make the shopper click. Competes against 20-60 products on the SERP.

**Hard requirements:** White background (#FFFFFF), product fills 85%+, no text/logos/badges, min 1000px (2000x2000 recommended), actual product only.

**Creative levers:** Wide-angle/dramatic angles, product + premium packaging, on-product elements (tags, labels, stickers), packaging text legibility at thumbnail, soft shadows/depth of field.

**Process:** Audit current → Generate 5-10 concepts → Narrow to 2-3 → Produce → A/B test → Measure CTR.

**Scoring (6 dimensions, 1-10):** Fidelity, Background, Scroll-Stop, Compliance, Creative, Quality. Pass = average 7+ with no dimension below 5.

**Auto-fail triggers:** Product too small, flat catalog shot, dull lighting, cluttered multi-pack, visible cutout artifacts, product blending into white.

### Listing Images (Slots 2-7) — Close the Sale

**Job:** Convert browsers into buyers. Shoppers spend more time on images than bullets or A+.

**Slot priority:** Slots 2-3 = highest-impact conversion images (benefit photography). Slots 4-6 = supporting content. Slot 7 = strong close (never waste).

**Image types (priority order):** Benefit Photography → Lifestyle → Product Detail → Infographic → Comparison → Social Proof.

**Stack decision process:** List top benefits (from reviews + keywords) → List top questions/objections (from reviews + Rufus) → Identify table stakes (from competitor audit) → Rank by impact → Assign to slots by priority.

**Assessment (4 layers):** Design & Readability → Slot Effectiveness → Research Alignment → Conversion Impact (Keep/Revise/Replace).

**Infographic rules:** One message per image, 3-5 callouts max, 24pt+ text, photography dominates, copy is 3-6 words per callout.

### A+ Content — Expand the Story

**Job:** Reinforce purchase decision for deep-scrolling shoppers. 5-7 vertical modules.

**Two-half architecture:**
- **Top half (segments 1-3):** Capture & Convert. Photography-forward, emotionally compelling. Segment 1 = hero moment. NOT for specs/FAQs. Builds on listing images, doesn't repeat them.
- **Bottom half (segments 4+):** Inform & Reassure. Visual FAQ. Answer secondary questions, handle objections, provide details.

**Key rules:** Don't repeat listing images. Full-width > side-by-side on mobile. One message per segment. Scannable in 15-20 seconds. No carousels (vertical scroll only).

**Alt text is critical SEO:** Pack every A+ image alt text with product name, keywords, benefits, use case, target audience. Indexed by Amazon search + Rufus.

**A+ Premium goal:** Bespoke mobile layout, video capability, maximum visual impact.

## 4. Decision Rules & Matrices

### Research-to-Content Rules

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Benefit in 30%+ of reviews | MUST be photographically demonstrated |
| Recurring complaint | An image must preempt it |
| High-volume keyword for benefit not shown | CVR leak — fix immediately |
| Every competitor shows X, you don't | Table stake — match it |
| No competitor shows X, customers care about it | Differentiation opportunity (after basics) |
| Rufus FAQ that can be answered visually | Answer in image or A+ segment |

### Metric-to-Action Matrix

| Signal | Diagnosis | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Low CTR | Main image doesn't stop scroll | Redesign main image |
| High CTR + Low CVR | Listing doesn't convert clickers | Fix listing images / A+ |
| High returns | Product doesn't match expectations | Fix color accuracy, size representation |
| Negative reviews re visuals | "Looks different" / "Smaller than pictured" | Add scale references, honest photography |
| Low A+ engagement | Shoppers skip A+ | A+ is text-heavy, repetitive, or not compelling |

### Optimization Priority Order

1. Remove conversion barriers (unreadable text, misleading content)
2. Address high-volume keyword gaps (benefit searched but not shown)
3. Answer unanswered customer questions
4. Match competitive table stakes
5. Amplify purchase triggers
6. Create differentiation (only after basics covered)

### Common Mistakes (Ranked by Impact)

1. Too much text
2. Telling instead of showing
3. Wrong demographic
4. Generic stock photography
5. Ignoring mobile
6. Inconsistent design
7. Feature dumping (specs over outcomes)
8. Dark, cluttered, overwhelming
9. Wasting Slot 7
10. Copying a template

- **Question framing, sample size, significance** — the 7 rules for
  poll questions, 50-per-option sample-size rule, 50/60/70/95%
  confidence interpretation: `03-testing-methodology/question-framing.md`
- **ProductPinion MCP contract** — the tool surface Florence calls:
  `05-productpinion/mcp-contract.md`
- **Tool taxonomy** — Pinion Videos vs Polls vs Ask:
  `05-productpinion/tool-taxonomy.md`
- **Case studies** — six wins Florence references:
  `05-productpinion/case-studies.md`

Headline rules:
1. **50 respondents per option** is the default sample size. Top up
   on a live test rather than relaunching when the read is tight.
2. **70%+ confidence** is shippable. 60–69% is a top-up zone. 50% means
   the variants are too similar — bigger swings, not more respondents.
3. **No result IS a result.** A null finding tells the seller to try a
   more distinct concept, not to keep relaunching the same test.
4. **Default to a continuous testing loop**, not a one-shot test.

## Category Quick Reference

| Category | Main Image | Listing Focus | A+ Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Beauty/Skincare** | Front label, color accuracy | Texture, application, ingredients, before/after | Ingredient story, skin type, routine |
| **Supplements** | Front label (not Supp Facts) | Lab testing, supplement facts, sourcing, dosage | Clinical backing, sourcing, usage |
| **Electronics** | Product off, clean angle | Feature in action, compatibility, what's in box | Setup, compatibility, specs |
| **Kitchen/Home** | Product as displayed/stored | Use case, lifestyle, dimensions, materials | Multiple uses, care, material story |
| **Fashion/Apparel** | On model or flat lay | Fit, fabric detail, size chart, color options | Fabric story, fit guide, styling |
| **Pet Products** | Product only (no pets) | Pet using it, size by breed, safety certs | Size/breed guide, safety, durability |
| **Food/Grocery** | Packaging visible | Recipe ideas, sourcing, nutrition | Origin story, quality, dietary info |
| **Outdoor/Garden** | Strong presentation | In setting, weather proof, dimensions | Durability, seasonal use, setup |
