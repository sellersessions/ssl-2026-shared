Most people in this room haven't heard of these

LoRA.

A technique to train a model with your own data.

LoRA = Low-Rank Adaptation. Take a huge pre-trained model, add a small adapter trained on your data.

  • Low-Rank = an efficient shortcutthrough the model's weight space — not a full retrain, a targeted nudge.
  • Adaptation = steering, not buildingyou're bending an existing model, not constructing one from scratch.
  • Text (LLMs) Brand voice, category knowledge, buyer psychology, product expertise.
  • Images (FLUX / Stable Diffusion) Visual style, product look, brand aesthetic — consistent imagery at scale.

How ChatGPT got good

The unlock that made ChatGPT useful

Training via polls.

Sam Altman
Lex Fridman Podcast · #367

The simplest version of this is show two outputs, ask which one is better than the other, which one the human raters prefer, and then feed that back into the model with reinforcement learning.

Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) is the feedback loop that made ChatGPT feel useful.

ProductPinion polls are the same type of preference data.

You've been collecting training data without realising it.
